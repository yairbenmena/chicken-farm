# 🐔 Yahav's Chicken Farm

A single-player browser game. Help **Yahav** run a farm full of dark and white chickens:

1. 🌾 Feed and 💧 water the chickens so they lay eggs (dark hens lay black eggs, white hens lay white eggs).
2. 🧺 Collect the eggs and sort them into the black pile and the white pile.
3. 🚚 Ship customer orders for coins and buy more feed, water and hens.
4. 🔥 Put eggs in the incubator, keep them warm, hatch chicks and raise them into new chickens.

Finish all missions to win the Golden Egg 🏆. Progress is saved in your browser.

Play: open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repo.
